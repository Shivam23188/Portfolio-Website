02 — About

I studied how software is built. Somewhere in second year, I got more curious about how it feels to use.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Engineering, but design pulled me in early — redesigning college apps I found frustrating, reading everything I could, and turning those experiments into proper case studies.

Since then I've designed for a startup as an intern and for clients as a freelancer. My engineering background means I design with constraints in mind, write handoff notes developers thank me for, and never hand over a screen I don't understand how to build.

I'm now looking for a full-time product design role on a team that cares about research, craft and shipping.

03 — How I work

A simple process, done carefully.

Not a rigid framework — just the habits that keep me honest about who I'm designing for and whether it actually works.

01

Understand

Talk to users and stakeholders, map the current journey, and find the real problem behind the request.

02

Define

Frame the problem, set success metrics, and agree on scope before a single pixel is pushed.

03

Design

Sketch wide, then narrow. Wireframes, flows and prototypes — tested with real people early and often.

04

Deliver

Clean files, documented components and close work with developers until it ships — then measure.

Good design isn't about making things pretty. It's about making the right thing easy and the wrong thing hard.
— The principle I design by
