Aarogya Clinic: booking without the phone call.

A mobile-first website and appointment flow for a neighbourhood clinic — designed for patients of every age and every level of smartphone comfort.

Role
Freelance designer
Timeline
4 weeks
Tools
Figma, Framer
Platform
Responsive website

On this page

Overview Problem Research Design Accessibility Outcome Learnings
Overview

The short version

The clinic had no website — just a phone number that was always busy between 9 and 11 am. The doctor asked for "a simple site". What patients actually needed was a way to book without calling, in a language they were comfortable with.

Problem

Jammed phone lines

Patients gave up and went to other clinics.

Solution

3-step booking

Doctor, time slot, name & number. That's it.

Result

Live & in use

Receptionist now manages bookings from a simple list.

The problem

A website wasn't the real brief

After a day at the reception desk, it was clear the brief was not "make us look professional online" but "stop losing patients to a busy phone line". That reframing shaped every decision after.

"How might we let patients book an appointment in under a minute, without needing help?"
Research

Who's actually booking?

  • Many bookings were made by adult children for their parents — often from another city.
  • Older patients preferred Hindi and larger text.
  • Nobody wanted to create an account just to see a doctor.
  • The receptionist needed something simpler than any off-the-shelf booking software.
Design

Three steps, no account

Choose doctor→Pick a slot→Name & phone→WhatsApp confirmation
  • Big, tappable time slots grouped by morning / evening instead of a calendar picker.
  • Hindi / English toggle at the top of every page, remembered on return.
  • Booking for someone else as a first-class option, not an afterthought.
  • Confirmation on WhatsApp, where patients already are — no email needed.
Accessibility

Designed for the least confident user

Readability

18px base text

High-contrast colours meeting WCAG AA across all screens.

Touch

48px tap targets

Generous spacing so slots are hard to mis-tap.

Language

Plain words

"Pick a time" instead of "Select appointment slot".

Forgiveness

Easy to change

Every step has a clear back button; nothing is lost.

Outcome

What changed

0

steps from landing page to confirmed booking

0

languages supported from day one

0

accounts or passwords required

Learnings

Working directly with a client

  • The first brief is rarely the real one — spending a day on-site was the best investment of the project.
  • Showing a clickable prototype got faster, clearer feedback than any static mockup.
  • Designing for the least confident user made the experience better for everyone.
